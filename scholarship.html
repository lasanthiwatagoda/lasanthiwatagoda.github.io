Current Research Focus

A central goal of my research is to develop reliable methods for statistical inference after variable selection.

In regression problems, many predictor variables may be available for predicting a response. Variable-selection methods seek a useful subset of those predictors while retaining the information needed for accurate estimation and prediction. My work investigates how uncertainty can be appropriately quantified after this selection process, with particular emphasis on bootstrap methods and prediction intervals.

I am also interested in actuarial science and interdisciplinary applications of statistical and machine-learning methods.
